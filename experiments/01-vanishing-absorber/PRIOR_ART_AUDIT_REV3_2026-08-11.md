## 1. Wavelength-dependent photodiode RF phase is established

Glasser et al., *Optics Express* 29(13), 19839-19852 (2021), DOI `10.1364/OE.424157`, explicitly uses wavelength-dependent photodiode response / optoelectronic chromatic dispersion for wavelength monitoring.

Therefore no manuscript claim should imply that wavelength-dependent absorption depth producing RF phase is new.

## 2. Hankel photodetector model identification is established

Sun et al., *Optics Express* 33(7), 15125-15140 (2025), DOI `10.1364/OE.550721`, applies Hankel-matrix system identification to a **time-sampled photodetector impulse-response sequence**.

Therefore no manuscript claim should imply that Hankel/model-order identification of a photodetector is new.

The candidate distinction remains the sequence coordinate: the present paper builds its finite-dimensional sequence from **spectrally selected internal source position**, after Shockley-Ramo-aware spatial differencing, and then constrains the recovered spatial roots across RF frequency.
